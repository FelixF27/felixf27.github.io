Hi, I'm Felix Fuentes III

Atmospheric Science & Computer Science Student | Machine Learning for Weather and Coastal Hazards

I'm double majoring in Atmospheric Science and Computer Science at Texas A&M University–Corpus Christi, with a minor in Applied Mathematics. I build machine learning models that turn weather and water data into forecasts people can act on, from coastal flooding in Corpus Christi to hurricane impacts at the National Hurricane Center. I graduate in May 2027 and am interested in work that connects machine learning with weather, climate, and public safety. This is a test!

Projects

Coastal Compound Flooding Prediction

A multilayer perceptron (MLP) model that predicts coastal compound flooding for Corpus Christi, Texas, developed at the Conrad Blucher Institute with NSF funding. I'm working with the National Weather Service to move it toward operational forecasting, and a manuscript is in preparation.

Hurricane Impact Forecasting

Machine learning models built during my NOAA Hollings internship at the National Hurricane Center that forecast county-level power outages, restoration time, compound heat and outage risk, and damage from landfalling hurricanes. They help forecasters show emergency managers which counties are affected and why.

Sea-Breeze Convection Prediction

Machine learning code for National Weather Service Corpus Christi research on predicting sea-breeze showers and thunderstorms. I built the training dataset by analyzing radar imagery by hand.
